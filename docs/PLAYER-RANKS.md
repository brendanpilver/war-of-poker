## Established Principle

> **WHO = Rank + Style + Situation**

This is the composition of the **W** step of WARPLAN — see
[`WARPLAN.md` § W](./WARPLAN.md#w--who--profile-the-player).

The military rank characters represent recognizable poker player types.

## Rules for Agents and Contributors

1. Do not invent ranks, characters, names, or archetypes. Not even as examples.
2. Do not infer the roster from the military rank structure of any real armed
   force.
3. When content needs a character that does not exist yet, stop and flag it.
4. Character behaviour must stay consistent with whatever is written here and in
   [`BRAND.md`](./BRAND.md); if content requires a contradiction, flag it rather
   than editing this file.
