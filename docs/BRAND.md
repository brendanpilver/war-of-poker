The motif is **instructional military**, not combat military: field manuals,
briefing rooms, tactical diagrams, insignia, and vintage instructional graphics —

## Brand Do's

- Treat War of Poker as the world and WARPLAN as the system inside it.
- Keep strategic material useful, tactical, organized, credible, and
  field-manual-like.
- Keep characters cartoonish, distinctive, memorable, slightly exaggerated, and fun.
- Quote canonical phrases exactly, including
  **"Change the price; change the range."**
- Draw on vintage military *instructional* graphics.

## Brand Don'ts

The brand should **not** feel like:

- generic military cosplay
- violent warfare propaganda
- generic AI-generated poker branding
- casino gambling hype
- macho poker-bro branding

Also:

- Do not rename, reinterpret, or paraphrase WARPLAN terminology.
- Do not present WARPLAN as the brand.
- Do not generate placeholder or filler artwork and treat it as brand assets.
- Do not choose fonts or colors before they are established here.
