## Governing Principle — Canonical

> **AI-generated War of Poker content must derive from canonical doctrine rather
> than inventing or silently changing the doctrine.**

Consequences of this principle, which apply now:

1. Content is written **from** [`WAR-OF-POKER-SPEC.md`](./WAR-OF-POKER-SPEC.md),
   [`BRAND.md`](./BRAND.md), [`WARPLAN.md`](./WARPLAN.md), and
   [`PLAYER-RANKS.md`](./PLAYER-RANKS.md) — not alongside them.
2. If a piece of content needs doctrine that does not exist yet, the content stops
   and the gap is raised. The content does not invent the doctrine.
3. If a piece of content would be better with different doctrine, that is a
   proposal to the project owner, never an edit made in passing.
4. Canonical terminology appears verbatim in content. No synonyms, no renaming, no
   "clearer" rewordings of WARPLAN terms.

## AI Agent Responsibilities

TODO: The per-stage split between agents and humans.

Established now, regardless of the final design:

- Agents read the doctrine files before producing War of Poker content.
- Agents do not edit doctrine to make content easier to write.
- Agents surface doctrine gaps and conflicts explicitly instead of papering over
  them.
- Agents do not invent War of Poker intellectual property — no new ranks,
  characters, WARPLAN terms, product names, or canonical phrases.
- Agents preserve canonical phrasing exactly, including
  **"Change the price; change the range."**
